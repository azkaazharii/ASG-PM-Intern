# VS SAD - Document Sign & Report Monitoring

## Overview
This module is a functional enhancement for the VS SAD (Vertical Solution - Sales Administration) platform, designed to digitize and streamline document verification, signature tracking, and centralized operational reporting for legal documents (FRU, FPU, and Payment Schedule).

## Core Functions
* **Manual Crop & Signature Preview:** Allows users to manually crop signature areas on uploaded PDF documents and instantly review them by hovering over an eye icon without full file scrolling.
* **Dual-Tier Verification System:** Establishes distinct verification checkpoints—"Sudah Ditandatangani" (for staff) and "Sudah Diperiksa" (for supervisors/managers)—complete with automated timestamps and verifier identity tracking.
* **Real-Time Summary Statistics:** Displays aggregate progress metrics at the top of the page to monitor overall document completion and verification statuses at a glance.
* **Centralized Monitoring Dashboard:** Replaces scattered manual tracking with a global monitoring interface that summarizes reservation unit statuses transparently.
* **Advanced Multi-Level Filtering:** Provides interactive filter options (by project, company, marketing, status, confirmation date, and document check/sign status) to accelerate operational data lookups.
* **Integrated Report Export:** Enables structured, fast data exporting of document recapitulations directly into Excel spreadsheet formats via a dedicated report log.

## Summary of Changes (To-Be vs. As-Is)
* **Document Verification Workflow:** Replaced full-file manual scrolling with targeted manual cropping, instant hover-based previews, and role-based checkbox approvals.
* **Audit Trail & Governance:** Upgraded basic signed markers to a transparent two-tier verification workflow (Staff signature checks and Supervisor examination checks) bound to automated timestamps and user IDs.
* **Monitoring & Reporting:** Transitioned from separate manual records to a centralized monitoring module equipped with multi-level filtering and direct Excel download capabilities.

## Business Impact & Value
* **Enhanced Accuracy & Speed:** Drastically reduces signature verification friction and eliminates manual scrolling through instant visual previews and targeted cropping.
* **Strict Operational Governance:** Enforces clear segregation of duties between operational staff and management (Supervisor/Manager approval tiers) with verifiable audit trails.
* **Centralized Visibility & Reporting:** Streamlines administrative tracking and reporting tasks, transforming fragmented manual checks into an integrated, transparent, and easily exportable dashboard workflow.
