Agung Sedayu Group – Product Development
This repository contains a collection of prototypes and product improvements developed during my internship as a Product Management Intern at Agung Sedayu Group (ASG).

Throughout this experience, I worked on exploring and translating business needs into practical improvements for internal applications through interactive prototyping. The work covered a wide range of enhancements, from small usability refinements that simplify everyday interactions to the introduction of new features and workflow improvements designed to make existing processes faster and more efficient.

Some improvements focused on addressing seemingly minor inconveniences that could have a meaningful impact on users' daily activities. Others involved rethinking existing workflows, reducing unnecessary steps, improving information accessibility, and introducing new functionalities to support business operations.

The prototypes in this repository represent different stages of product exploration and development, illustrating how ideas and business requirements can be translated into tangible solutions before implementation.
Areas of Work
- User Experience Improvements: Refining existing interfaces and interactions to make everyday tasks simpler, clearer, and more intuitive.
- Feature Development: Exploring and designing new functionalities to address operational needs and improve existing application capabilities.
- Workflow Optimization: Identifying inefficient processes and proposing solutions to reduce manual work, unnecessary steps, and processing time.
- Business Requirements Translation: Turning stakeholder feedback and business needs into structured feature concepts and interactive prototypes.
Each prototype reflects an effort to make internal tools more practical, efficient, and aligned with the people who use them every day.
